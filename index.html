Privacy Policy for 2Night

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Welcome to 2Night. This Privacy Policy explains how 2Night collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use 2Night, we may collect:

  • Name and basic account information
  • Email address
  • Profile photo and other profile information
  • Date of birth and age
  • Height and weight
  • City or approximate location information
  • Interests and partner preferences
  • Relationship or dating preferences
  • Information you provide through chats and interactions
  • Information required to provide, secure, and improve the service

2. Login Information

2Night may allow you to sign in using third-party authentication providers such as Google and Facebook. When you use these services, we may receive information that the provider makes available to us, such as your name, email address, profile picture, and account identifier, depending on your permissions and provider settings.

3. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Create and display your profile
  • Help you discover and connect with other users
  • Enable likes, matches, and conversations
  • Provide and improve 2Night features
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Communicate with you about the service
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements

4. Profile Information

Information you choose to include in your profile may be visible to other 2Night users. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in your public profile or messages.

5. Location Information

2Night may use city or approximate location information to provide location-based discovery and matching features. We do not intend to display your precise device location publicly.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the application, authentication services, hosting providers, analytics providers, or when required by law.

7. Third-Party Services

2Night may use third-party services such as Google, Facebook, Supabase, and other technology providers to provide authentication, hosting, databases, storage, and other application functionality. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

10. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your 2Night account and associated personal information. Certain information may be retained when required by law or for legitimate security and legal purposes.

11. Children's Privacy

2Night is intended for adults and is not directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for using the service. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the date shown at the top of this page.

13. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, you can contact the 2Night team through the contact method provided in the application.
